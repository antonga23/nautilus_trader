Data is web-scraped and varies in reliability — each row carries a confidence dot (green/amber/red) and a source link. Verify a book's terms on its own site before depositing; bonus offers change often. Many entries are flagged casino-only (can't be hedged on a sports exchange) or out-of-region (non-ZAR) — hidden by default.

This is matched betting against the books' interests. Nearly every SA book voids bonuses for "irregular play": hedging bonus bets, covering both sides of a market, or risk-free profit-seeking. The clean EV here ignores that void risk, account limits, and exchange liquidity at each book's odds floor — haircut accordingly. Free-bet / no-deposit rows show net = full bonus, which is an upper bound (stake-not-returned free bets realistically retain ~70%).

Bets = ⌈turnover ÷ stake-per-bet⌉, assuming you stake at the book's max. Leave max stake blank to model "no cap" → clears in 1 large bet (bounded only by exchange liquidity, not EV). Base (deposit+bonus vs bonus-only) is the biggest lever — verify it first. Net kept assumes the deposit is recovered; profit = bonus − cost of turning it over. ROI shows "n/a" for no-deposit offers.
